Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about our partner and affiliate program:

1. Is there any registration fee to become a partner?
No, joining is 100% free. Create your account in 30 seconds, get your unique referral link and custom promo code, and start earning immediately. No upfront costs or hidden fees.
2. What is the minimum payout and how fast is it processed?
The minimum payout is just $20. You can request withdrawals anytime via Binance Pay, USDT (TRC-20), bKash, or Nagad. Payouts are reviewed and approved swiftly with full transaction proof.
3. How does referral tracking and cookie duration work?
When a visitor clicks your referral link, a 90-day tracking cookie is stored in their browser. If they sign up and buy any challenge within 90 days, you get credited. Additionally, if they enter your promo code at checkout, they are permanently linked to your account.
4. Do my referred traders get a discount?
Yes! Your default promo code provides your traders with an immediate 5% discount at checkout. As your sales grow, you can request custom promo codes with discounts up to 15%.
5. Will traders experience any broker conflicts or issues?
Not at all. Traders trade directly on their own personal broker accounts (Quotex, Pocket Option, Olymp Trade, Binomo, IQ Option). Binary Prop Firm is an evaluation and prop funding platform and never touches or requires broker logins.
6. Can my commission rate ever be downgraded?
Never. Once you reach a tier (such as Elite 50% or Master 55%), that rate is locked in permanently. Even if you have a month with fewer sales, your tier and commission rate are never downgraded.
